October 29, 2026

I’m outgrowing my own story.

I’ve been having a hard time showing up on social media.

That is a strange thing for me to admit because, for almost ten years, sharing has come naturally to me. I have let people into my businesses, my motherhood, my marriage and some of the hardest parts of my life.

I built The Mom Halo because I needed a place where mothers could be honest. Connection over perfection. For years, I knew exactly why I was showing up.

Lately, I open Instagram and don’t know what to say.

I know how to make content. I know how to write a hook, make a Reel and package something so people will respond to it. I just don’t know if I want to create something simply because I know it will perform.

I only want to share things that feel true and useful. Things that might actually help someone I care about. Oddly, wanting my work to matter more has made it harder to make anything at all.

I keep thinking about the difference between reach and impact. I don’t want to stay visible just for the sake of staying visible. I want to understand what I can genuinely offer women and where my voice can do the most good.

For a long time, motherhood was the main character of my story. It changed me, built The Mom Halo and connected me to thousands of women.

But I’m starting to wonder whether the motherhood main character is ready to take a step to the side.

Maybe she isn’t retiring completely but she just doesn’t have to carry the entire story anymore.

I feel like I have reached a new summit. I can see everything I climbed to get here, but I’m not certain the next part of the path looks the same.

I’ve been asking myself some uncomfortable questions:

If I weren’t afraid, what would I do?

If every decision were truly my own, what would I choose?

What is my actual gift?

And if I already had the life I say I want, what would an ordinary Tuesday look like?

I don’t have the answers yet.

Maybe I don’t want to quit, i’m simply tired of performing a version of myself that no longer feels complete.

So if I have seemed quieter, that is why. I’m thinking carefully about what deserves my voice and how I want to move through this next chapter.

I’m not disappearing. I’m listening.

And whatever comes next, I want it to be honest.

XO,
Alana
