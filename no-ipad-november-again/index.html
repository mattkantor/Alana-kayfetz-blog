No iPad November, Again!

We’re Doing No iPad November Again!

This month, our family is putting the iPads away.

Not because I think technology is evil, and definitely not because I believe I have parenting figured out. I have four kids. Sometimes an iPad is the reason I can make dinner, answer an email or hear myself think.

But last November, we tried going without them for a month, and the change in our house was impossible to ignore.

At first, I expected constant complaining. Instead, they barely missed them.

They played differently. They started reading. They listened to music, and one of them proudly announced, “Music is my new iPad.” They went to bed earlier, had fewer emotional outbursts and somehow became kinder and funnier. Their teachers even noticed an improvement in their reading fluency.

And they cuddled us more.

It honestly felt like we got our kids back.

I don’t think the iPads were turning them into bad kids. I think they were filling every quiet moment before boredom had the chance to become imagination, conversation or connection.

So we’re doing it again.

I know it won’t be perfect. There will be complaining, long afternoons and moments when handing over a screen would make my life much easier. This is not a moral stance or a judgment of how anyone else manages their household. It is simply a reset our family needs.

For one month, we’re making a little more room for boredom. For books. For music. For fighting over toys, figuring it out and playing together again.

Mostly, we’re making more room for each other.

I’ll let you know how it goes.
